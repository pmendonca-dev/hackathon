# Decision Flight Log

Continuously capture concise, faithful records of material decisions made during this project. The entry must describe a choice that was actually made; it is not a work diary or a list of implementation steps.

Remain active for the entire task. Monitor the requirements, discussion, investigation, and implementation as they evolve; do not wait for the user to ask for a log entry. As soon as a material choice is finalized, create its Flight Log entry before moving on to the next independent decision or finishing the task.

## Identify decisions

Treat a choice as material when it affects the project's scope, user experience, architecture, data, integrations, security, performance, delivery constraints, or a meaningful trade-off. A decision can come from the user, the team, or a substantiated implementation choice.

A choice is finalized when it is explicitly approved, selected by the user or team, or committed to in the implementation. Do not delay logging a finalized decision until the end of the task.

Do not log:

- Requirements that leave no meaningful alternative.
- Routine mechanical actions, trivial refactors, or ordinary tool usage.
- Provisional ideas, assumptions presented as facts, or choices that are still unresolved.
- Alternatives or reasons that were not actually considered or supported by the task context.

If a task contains independent decisions, create one Flight Log entry per decision. Do not combine unrelated choices just to reduce the number of entries.

## Build each entry

Use the exact fields shown in the Flight Log form. Keep the content concise. All text entered in the Flight Log, including copy-ready values, must be in English regardless of the language used in the request or project discussion.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Decision** | A neutral statement of the subject being decided, not a status update. |
| **Options considered (one per line)** | Each viable option genuinely considered, one per line. Do not pad this list with invented options. |
| **What we chose** | The single selected option, stated directly. |
| **Why** | The evidence, requirement, constraint, and trade-offs that justify the choice. State an important assumption only when it materially affects the decision. |

Before logging, verify that the selected option and rationale agree with the work that was actually performed or explicitly approved.

## Fill the Flight Log

When the Flight Log form is available and recording is within the current task's authorization, immediately populate its four fields after each finalized material decision. Submit one entry at a time only after all fields are complete and accurate; then resume the task.

When the form is unavailable or submission is not authorized, provide copy-ready values under the four field labels instead. Never claim that an entry was submitted unless it was actually recorded.
